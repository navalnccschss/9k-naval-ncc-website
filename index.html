About Us

The Naval Wing of the National Cadet Corps (NCC) at St. Catherine's Higher Secondary School, Payyampally, was inaugurated on 19 January 2024 by Commander Mathew P. Mathew, the then Commanding Officer of 9(K) Naval Unit NCC, under the guidance of Associate NCC Officer Sub Lieutenant Sheril Thomas.

Our unit aims to develop discipline, leadership, patriotism and social responsibility among students and shape them into responsible citizens and future leaders of our nation.
